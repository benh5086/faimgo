Your side-income GPS

Not nine books to read — one map of moves, and a system that picks your next step and reroutes you when a better road opens.

How to read it: each lane is a path, each dot is one small move (a "play"). Your route lights up in color, the ring is your next move, and a gold dashed line is a reroute — the GPS sending you to a faster or bigger road. Pick a goal below and watch the route change.
